01About

I'm a mechanical engineer who likes to model a system properly before building it. I spent two years at Dassault Systèmes helping automotive, truck and motorsport teams adopt Simpack for multibody simulation. Before that I captained Team Redshift Racing through a Baja SAE season. Now at Penn, I'm extending that work into robotics, controls and MEMS.

A

Simulation first

Multibody models in Simpack and Adams, FEA in ANSYS, topology optimization in Inspire, all before anything gets cut.

B

Built, not just modeled

Lathe, mill, water jet, laser cutter and 3D printer. I've built everything from Baja uprights to robot chassis.

C

Leads teams

Captained 45 engineers to a top 2 Baja finish and won funding from 8 industry partners.
